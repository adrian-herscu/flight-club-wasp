# Project Brief

## Project
Flight Club Platform

## Summary
A multi-role flight school management platform built with Wasp, Prisma, PostgreSQL, React, and TypeScript.

## Core Goals
- Enable public discovery of flight schools and courses.
- Support registration and approval workflows for role elevation.
- Provide school management workflows (profiles, syllabuses, courses, memberships).
- Keep behavior covered by API and E2E tests.

## Scope
- App logic in Wasp app under app/.
- API-level tests under api-tests/.
- E2E flows under e2e-tests/.
- Documentation/blog under blog/ and top-level docs.

## Success Criteria
- Core role-based workflows are reliable and tested.
- Schema and operations remain aligned with main.wasp and schema.prisma.
- Team can resume work quickly from memory-bank context.
